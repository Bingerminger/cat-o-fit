## Understand it in 2 minutes

Cat-O-Fit plans and supports the training of your whole family – with a race or with none at all.
The common thread is always the same:

**Goal → Plan → Calendar → Training → Evaluation → Progress**

You set up a goal – a **race** (e.g. the Dresden Half Marathon) or a **training programme** without a
race –, automatically get a plan for it, train by the calendar and see how you are developing. The
app saves every change on your device straight away and syncs it with your own server or NAS; without
a connection, simply later – even in the middle of a run.

> 💡 **Tip:** In Safari, use the Share icon to “Add to Home Screen”. The app then launches
> full-screen like a real app.

> 🌍 **Seven languages:** Cat-O-Fit speaks German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian
> Portuguese and Dutch. On a new instance the app starts in your browser's language (English if
> yours is not among the seven). Each person can choose their own language under
> **Settings → Appearance → Language** – it applies to you on every device. Admins also set the
> instance's **Default language** (in the admin-only “Administration (admin)” section of Settings)
> for the sign-in screen and for new people. In the same section, under **Units**, everyone picks
> kilometres or miles, kilograms or pounds, °C or °F, the first day of the week and the lab units – at
> first the app follows your browser's region (miles, pounds and °F in the United States).

## The first five minutes

1. **First-time setup:** The very first time you open the app, an assistant (in your browser's
   language) creates the admin person – a name and your own PIN (4 to 8 digits, not `0000`). Then you
   choose **“Start with demo data”** (a sample family to try things out) or **“Start empty”**.
2. **Sign in:** Tap your tile and enter your PIN. More under
   [Team & family](family.md#teamfamily--signing-in).
3. **Set up a goal:** More → “Goals & plans” → “+” → **Race** or **Training programme** – see
   [Planning training](training.md#set-up-a-race--create-a-plan).
4. **Train:** On “Today” you find the day's session; ▶ starts workout mode, “Log as done” records a
   session afterwards.
5. **Get it onto your iPhone:** Open the address in Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”.

## Navigation & controls

**iPhone – the bar at the bottom:** **Today** · **Calendar** · **＋ Log** · **Progress** · **More**.

- **＋ Log** is the fixed place for everything you enter quickly: a **Training** (even without a
  plan), **Body stats**, a **Meal**, a **Lab value**, the start of a **Period** or a **Checklist
  item** – depending on which modules are on. It saves into the page you are on; that page stays
  where it is.
- **Progress** bundles four tabs: **Training** (statistics, form & training zones), **Body** (body
  values), **Badges** and **Reports**.
- **More** is organised into groups – Training · Health · Food & household · Team · System – and
  marks where you are. At the top it always shows **who is signed in**, with **Sign out**.

**iPad & Mac – the sidebar:** **＋ Log** at the top, below it Today, Calendar, Progress and the
groups. The **footer is always visible**: the signed-in person plus **Settings · Help · Sign out**.
Forms open as a dialog in the middle of the screen. In landscape, “Today” shows two columns, and the
month calendar names sessions by a short title (“Long 14 km”) instead of just coloured dots.

**Managing a member:** When an admin opens another profile, a banner at the top reads **“You are
managing Lea”** with **“Back to me”** – the same note appears in the More menu, in the sidebar and
in Settings. “Today” is then called **“Lea’s overview”**.

**Saving without reloading:** After saving, the page stays where you were and the confirmation
(“Saved”) stays readable. Deleted entries (checklist, food diary) and moving a session by dragging in
the calendar can be **undone** straight from the notice.

**Accessibility:** Zooming is allowed; text and colours reach WCAG AA contrast – with every accent
colour, light and dark. On a Mac, **“Skip to content”** (the first Tab stop) takes you straight to the
content, **Escape** closes dialogs, and the arrow keys choose in toggles. Screen readers hear a
summary for every chart (period, lowest, highest and latest value).

**Without a connection:** The app starts instantly from the device's storage and syncs in the
background. If the server does not answer (when you are out and about, or the server is asleep), you
carry on working offline – your changes go out as soon as it is reachable again. After you sign in, a
link from a calendar reminder takes you straight to the session.

## Help inside the app

Under **More → Help & knowledge** you find guides and training knowledge, with search – it also
knows technical terms like “RED-S”, “ACWR” or “sRPE”. Next to figures such as **Load & form**,
**Current form**, **Energy availability** or the **Heart rate zones** there is a small **ⓘ**: it
explains the number in two or three sentences, and “Open in Help” leads to the full article. At the
end of the help there is a **Glossary** with all the terms.

## Reading values from a chart

<img src="../assets/en/08-body-values.png" width="260" align="right" alt="Body values with scrubber tooltip" />

Every curve in Cat-O-Fit gives you **concrete numbers** – you do not have to guess what it is
showing right now.

1. Run your **finger** across the chart (on a computer, the mouse).
2. A guide line jumps to the nearest data point, and a small bubble shows the **date and value**.
3. With several curves – such as **fitness, fatigue and form** – you see all the values for the same
   day at once, colour-coded.
4. On a computer it also works **by keyboard**: move with the arrow keys, close with Escape.

> 💡 The **y-axis** shows round reference values with hairline grid lines – so the range is visible
> before you touch the chart. Vertical scrolling on the iPhone stays free; the chart does not trap
> your finger.
